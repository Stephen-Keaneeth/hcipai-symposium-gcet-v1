The HCIPAI Inception

The Human-Computer Interaction Professionals Association of India convenes its flagship 2027 symposium to challenge deterministic computing. Through experiential prototypes, multi-sensory feedback loops, and cognitive architecture, our symposium fosters dialogues that place collective human wellbeing at the nexus of technology.

Explore Association Mission

Interactive Systems & Generative AI

TRACK 01

Investigating adaptive conversational agents, predictive gesture systems, dynamic ergonomics, and human-in-the-loop interfaces designed for enterprise and accessibility applications.

Immersive AR/VR & Multimodal Interfaces

TRACK 02

From spatial telepresence and haptic arrays to cross-reality simulations, dissecting how immersive sensory cues influence memory retention, collaboration, and spatial awareness.

Inclusive & Cultural Design in Tech

TRACK 03

Rooting technological interactions in contextual semantics, indigenous design languages, vernacular typography, and ethical paradigms celebrating South Asian socio-technical diversity.
